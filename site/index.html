Videos play in the side panel with one click, seeking included. Anything the built-in player can’t handle opens in VLC.

RAW files from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm and others are catalogued with their camera, lens and exposure today. Their previews come in a later version.

Finds the copy you made in 2019 and forgot about.

Hundreds of thousands of files, compared without reading most of them.

  1. Same size?

    Only files that share their exact size with another file are candidates. Everything else is never opened.

  2. Same start and end?

    A quick fingerprint of the first and last 64 KB rules out almost all of the rest.

  3. Same everything?

    Files that still match are read in full and compared byte for byte, with BLAKE3.

Similar photos, the resized, re-exported and recompressed ones, are matched from their thumbnails. That works even for drives that aren’t plugged in.

Nothing is deleted outright. Extras go to your system trash, where you can restore them, and Rummage won’t let the last copy go.

Easy on old hard drives.

Each physical disk gets its own reader: a few threads for an SSD, a single one for a spinning disk, so two partitions of one hard drive take turns instead of fighting over it.

Thumbnails and photo details appear in the background while you browse, and you can pause at any time.

Your catalog stays on your computer.

No account, no cloud, no uploading. The catalog and its thumbnails live in a folder you choose. The only time Rummage goes online is to check GitHub for a new version.

Rummage for Linux and Windows.

An AppImage that runs on any 64-bit Linux, a .deb for Ubuntu and Debian, and an installer for Windows. Updates install themselves after you say yes.
