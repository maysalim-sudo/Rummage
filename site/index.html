What it does

Built for a library too big to remember

Every drive, one library

Internal and external drives sit side by side. A drive is recognized at any letter or mount path, on Windows and Linux.

Browse what isn't plugged in

Thumbnails and details stay in the catalog, so an offline drive is as searchable as the one in front of you.

Photos first

A justified grid shows every picture in its real shape, grouped by month, with a timeline to jump through years in a drag.

Opens the files others can't

Previews for HEIC, PSD, AI, EPS, PDF, AVIF, EXR and Figma, and videos that play with seeking. Anything else opens in VLC.

Find copies and lookalikes

Exact copies are matched by content and similar photos by appearance. Extras go to the trash, and the last copy always stays.

Easy on old hard drives

Each physical disk works at its own pace, in the background, and you can pause it. Tested on 340,000 files.

Privacy

Your catalog stays on your computer

No account, no cloud and no uploading. The catalog and its thumbnails live in a folder you choose. The only time Rummage goes online is to check GitHub for a new version.

Install

Up and running in a few minutes

  1. Install it

    Run the Windows installer, which needs no admin rights, or use the AppImage or .deb on Linux. Windows may warn you first, because the installer isn't code-signed.

  2. Choose a catalog folder

    Pick a fast drive with room to spare. Thumbnails for a large library take a few gigabytes.

  3. Add your drives

    Rummage scans them, then fills in thumbnails and details while you browse.
